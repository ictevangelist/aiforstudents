# 5 C's of Digital Cognition

Source: Mark Anderson & Olly Lewis 2024 (The EdTech Playbook), ICT Evangelist graphic, CC BY-NC-SA. Transcribed verbatim for chapter 4. The published definitions are teacher-facing; the guide will need student-facing versions written by Mark or approved by him.

Diagram structure: "Digital Cognition" at the centre; Confidence, Competence, Consistency and Cognisance as the four quadrants around it; Context as the outer ring enclosing everything.

- **Confidence** - I am confident with my use of technology.
- **Competence** - I am competent in my use of technology.
- **Cognisance** - I am cognisant of what works with technology for teaching and learning.
- **Consistency** - I am using technology consistently with students, with colleagues in my team and across the school.
- **Context** - I am using technology within the confines of my context and school ecosystem.

Guide emphasis (Mark's steer): Cognisance is the most important C. It is about making informed choices about when to use, or not use, AI and technology, because sometimes not using it is the best choice.
