# A Student's Guide to Using AI

Free download guide for secondary students, pitched at a literate 14 year old, suitable 11-19. Portrait A4, 6-10 pages per chapter plus full-page infographics rotated 90 degrees. Fully accessible: tagged PDF, real reading order, alt text throughout, contrast-checked, dual coding with clear iconography. Microsite to follow at aiforstudents.ictevangelist.com.

## Voice

Mark's voice adapted for students: plain English, warm, direct second person, contractions fine, references students will understand. No AI mannerisms of any kind.

## Design

Fresh identity, faithful to the ICT Evangelist brand but distinct. Fat modern headings, light body text, Lexend a candidate for body. Keep the ribbon rainbow from the Pedagogy First guide. One icon per chapter, dual-coded diagrams that carry the idea.

## Chapters

1. **What AI actually is.** Plain-English mechanics: it predicts, it doesn't know. Trained on human writing so it inherits human patterns.
2. **When it gets things wrong.** Hallucinations: why they happen, why they sound confident, fluency is not accuracy, how to check.
3. **Bias.** Built on Mark's Types of Bias in AI Outputs infographic (see bias-types.md), recreated natively as a full spread. Key insight: bias is easy to see in image outputs, much harder to see in text.
4. **Digital cognition: the five Cs.** Confidence, competence, cognisance, consistency, context. Cognisance is the heart: the informed choice to use or not use AI. Credit Olly Lewis and The EdTech Playbook; Mark's five Cs graphic to come.
5. **AI in your subjects.** English, Maths, Science, History, Geography, Languages, Arts, Technology. What good use looks like in each, where AI harms the learning.
6. **The universal approaches.** What works everywhere. Draws on Ethan Mollick with credit, expositions written fresh. Includes Mark's STAIR prompting approach (material to come from Mark).

Every chapter ends with a "3 things to try today" / "3 takeaways" / "3 top tips" panel.
